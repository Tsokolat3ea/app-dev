# My Favorite Movie: Obsession



## About the Movie 
After breaking the mysterious "One Wish Willow" to win his crush's heart, a hopeless romantic finds himself getting exactly what he asked for but soon discovers that some desires come at a dark, sinister price.


## Main Characters 
- **Bear** – A shy guy who wishes for something he should have been more careful about.
- **Nikki** – The girl whose feelings for Bear change into something intense and frightening.


## My Favorite Part
My favorite part is how the story slowly builds from a normal love story into something creepy. I like how the tension keeps growing, so you never know what will happen next.


## My Rating
**10/10** – This movie is thrilling, creepy, and unforgettable. It is one of the most memorable movies I have ever watched.
